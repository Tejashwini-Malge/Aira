How far we've come

From the green board
to a coach that knows you.

Speaking practice has always been part of school. Only the tools changed.

The early 2000s
The green board era

Chalk squeaking, duster clouds, and "stand up and read your essay aloud." One teacher, forty students, and thirty seconds of feedback if you were lucky.

🖍️
The 2010s
Whiteboards & projectors

Markers replaced chalk, slides replaced handwriting. Group discussions and PowerPoint presentations — but feedback was still one-size-fits-all.

📽️
The 2020s
Screens & video calls

Classrooms moved into laptops. Recorded lectures, video interviews, online tests — practice anywhere, but still nobody listening to how you speak.

💻
Today · you are here
An AI that actually listens

Aira hears every word, scores your fluency, clarity and confidence, and coaches you one-on-one — the personal attention the green board era never had time for. Same classroom soul. A teacher for every single student.
